Transform Text

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026.

Transform Text has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. It doesn't store anything, and it doesn't talk to a server — every transformation happens locally, in the page, the moment you click.

Permissions

  • contextMenus — builds the right-click submenu.
  • activeTab — access only when you invoke the menu, only on that tab.
  • scripting — injects the transformed text into the field you selected it from.

No host_permissions — it can't read pages it isn't actively used on.

What Transform Text does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No advertising.
  • No cookies.
  • No data collection of any kind.

Questions

Transform Text is open source. If anything here is unclear, open an issue on GitHub.
